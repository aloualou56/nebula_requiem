Nebula Requiem 1.1 for Android: a Hangar laid out for phones, and scrolling that feels native.

**Download:** `nebula-requiem-1.1.apk` below. It installs over 1.0 and keeps your progress.

### What's new

**Hangar on phones and foldables**
- The top bar (Back, stardust, Launch) and the hull panel stay put; only the refit list scrolls, in its own pane
- Refits are compact cards with large buttons, in one column on phones and two on wide screens
- The hull panel fits the screen: preview, description, stats and the select or unlock button are all visible at once

**Scrolling everywhere**
- Flings follow the speed of your finger, measured from the touch screen's own timestamps
- Lifting a finger that has stopped no longer flings the page
- No jump when a drag starts; dragging past the top or bottom stretches a little and springs back
- A tap on a moving list just stops it instead of pressing whatever is under your finger
- A vertical swipe that starts on a settings slider scrolls the page instead of changing the volume
- A thin scroll bar shows while a page moves

### Requirements

- Android 13 or newer, in landscape; the only permission is vibration (no internet permission)

### Installing

Open the APK on the device and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when Android asks. It is signed with the same development key as 1.0, so it installs as an update. If the game ever stops unexpectedly, the next launch shows a report you can copy and send in.
