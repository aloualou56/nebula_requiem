Nebula Requiem 0.3 for Android: a campaign with an ending.

**Download:** `nebula-requiem-0.3.apk` below.

> **Uninstall the game first.** 0.3 is signed with a different key from 1.0, 1.1 and 1.1.1, so Android won't install it over them. Uninstalling deletes your save: stardust, refits, hulls, settings and records start over.

### New

- **Ten sectors, ten guardians.** Each sector is three waves, then its guardian. Helix Cantor, Lissajous Leviathan, Fractal Seraph and Entropy Engine hold sectors 1–4 as before.
- **Six new guardians** for sectors 5–10, each a piece of mathematics with its own movement, look and attacks: Fourier Orrery (epicycles), Penrose Pentarch (a tiling that never repeats), Trefoil Hierophant (torus knots), Mandelbrot Matriarch (the cardioid and Julia sets), Automaton Augur (cellular automata) and Euler Eidolon, a three-phase finale that quotes every guardian before it.
- **An ending.** Breaking the tenth guardian wins the run, in place of the endless Ascension loop. Your stardust is banked, the ship warps out, and an epilogue, a roll call of the ten guardians and the run's telemetry play before you return to the title.

Hostiles, hulls, grafts, refits, scoring, stardust and difficulty are unchanged. Because a sector now has three waves, Mitosis first appears in sector 2, and the six mutators arrive as you enter sectors 2–7.

The version number goes down from 1.1.1 on purpose.

### Requirements

- Android 13 or newer, in landscape; the only permission is vibration (no internet permission)

### Installing

Uninstall any earlier version, then open the APK on the device and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when Android asks. If the game ever stops unexpectedly, the next launch shows a report you can copy and send in.
