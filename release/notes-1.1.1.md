Nebula Requiem 1.1.1 for Android: a fix for pens and mice.

**Download:** `nebula-requiem-1.1.1.apk` below. It installs over 1.1 or 1.0 and keeps your progress.

### Fixed

- The first tap with a pen or mouse after using a finger (or the other way round) could press the control one row away, because switching input mode shows or hides touch-only rows (such as the Vibration toggle in Settings). The tap now lands on what was on screen, and the menu re-lays out right after.

Everything from 1.1 is included: the Hangar laid out for phones and foldables, and native-feeling scrolling.

### Requirements

- Android 13 or newer, in landscape; the only permission is vibration (no internet permission)

### Installing

Open the APK on the device and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when Android asks. It is signed with the same development key as 1.0 and 1.1, so it installs as an update. If the game ever stops unexpectedly, the next launch shows a report you can copy and send in.
