Nebula Requiem 0.4 for Android: guardians that fight like guardians, and an endless mode.

**Download:** `nebula-requiem-0.4.apk` below. It installs over 0.3 and keeps your progress.

> **Coming from 1.1.1 or earlier?** Like 0.3, this version is signed with a different key from 1.0, 1.1 and 1.1.1, so Android won't install it over them. Uninstall the old version first; that deletes its save.

### New

- **Tougher guardians.** A guardian's HP used to grow only 6% a sector while the waves grew about tenfold over the campaign, so from sector 8 on a single wave had more HP than the guardian after it. Now every guardian grows at the waves' pace, with roughly twice the HP of its sector's biggest wave, and each one outlasts the one before. Deeper guardians also attack faster, 5% more each sector and 45% faster by sector 10, so their patterns get denser and their rests shorter.
- **Endless mode.** Launch run now opens a flight plan:
  - **Story** is the ten-sector campaign and its ending.
  - **Endless** never ends. Every sector is still three waves and a guardian, but the guardian is drawn at random from all ten. Each ten sectors deal every guardian once, and the same one never comes twice in a row. Every ten sectors the nebula ascends: hostiles and guardians harden, and they keep growing as you go deeper.
  - The flight plan screen shows how many requiems you've completed and your deepest endless sector.
  - It remembers your last choice, and Relaunch on the game-over screen flies the same plan again.

The waves keep their existing difficulty: later waves are harder than early ones exactly as before. Hostiles, hulls, grafts, refits, scoring and stardust are unchanged.

### Requirements

- Android 13 or newer, in landscape; the only permission is vibration (no internet permission)

### Installing

Open the APK on the device and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when Android asks. It is signed with the same key as 0.3, so it installs as an update and keeps your progress, including a run in progress. If the game ever stops unexpectedly, the next launch shows a report you can copy and send in.
