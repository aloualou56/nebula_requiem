Nebula Requiem 0.4.1 for Android: four difficulty levels, and eight new hostiles.

**Download:** `nebula-requiem-0.4.1.apk` below. It installs over 0.4 and 0.3 and keeps your progress. (This is the build that was briefly published as 0.3.1, renumbered; it installs over that too.)

> **Coming from 1.1.1 or earlier?** Like 0.3 and 0.4, this version is signed with a different key from 1.0, 1.1 and 1.1.1, so Android won't install it over them. Uninstall the old version first; that deletes its save.

### New

- **Difficulty levels.** The flight plan now asks how hard: Easy, Medium (the game exactly as it was in 0.4), Hard or Maniac. Harder levels bring tougher hostiles and more of them, faster fire and faster bullets, and guardians with more HP that attack faster; easier levels ease all of it off. The harder the level, the more score a run earns, and score converts to stardust: 0.75× on Easy, 1.3× on Hard, 1.7× on Maniac. Your choice is kept for the next launch, a run in progress keeps its level, and Relaunch flies the same level again.
- **Eight new hostiles.** Every sector from the third on brings a new kind of hostile in its first wave, right after you break a guardian:
  - **Prism** (sector 3): its shards fan out, stop dead, then all turn on you at once.
  - **Comet** (sector 4): sights a line through you, streaks along it and leaves a drifting wake.
  - **Pulsar** (sector 5): charges up, then fires a dense ring with one gap; its charge shows where the gap will be.
  - **Hive** (sector 6): keeps its distance and launches drones until its brood is spent.
  - **Vortex** (sector 7): winds a ring of shards around itself, then flings them all outward.
  - **Phantom** (sector 8): blinks out and reappears beside you (its arrival is marked first), then fires a burst. It can't be hit while it's away.
  - **Carom** (sector 9): banks its shots off the walls.
  - **Harbinger** (sector 10): a slow heavy that sweeps a beam through where you stand.

Guardians stay the toughest fight of every sector at every difficulty. Hulls, grafts, refits and the stardust formula are unchanged.

### Requirements

- Android 13 or newer, in landscape; the only permission is vibration (no internet permission)

### Installing

Open the APK on the device and allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps when Android asks. It is signed with the same key as 0.3 and 0.4, so it installs as an update and keeps your progress, including a run in progress. If the game ever stops unexpectedly, the next launch shows a report you can copy and send in.
